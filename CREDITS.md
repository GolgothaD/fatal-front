## Sound effects
Kenney (kenney.nl) sound packs, CC0, layered with original synthesized effects.

## Announcer
Generated with Piper TTS (voice en_US-ryan-medium), then processed into an arcade style.

## Art
The backgrounds and title art are AI-generated images, pixelated and palette-reduced for the game. Character sprites are procedurally drawn in code.

Fatal Front is an original game. It is not affiliated with any existing fighting or run-and-gun franchise.
